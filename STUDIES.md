## [THE ENVIRONMENT SUBSTRATE](The%20Environment%20Substrate/)

**Separate research manuscript · Seven chapters.** *The Environment as a Candidate Substrate for Conscious Organization* examines state, representation, memory, boundaries, and feedback, including a physical investigation of the Earth–ionosphere system.

This folder is an **archival assembly**: five chapter files were obtained from newer October 30, 2026 Library sources, while the Abstract, Chapters IV and VI, References, and About page come from the October 29 ZIP. Verify all chapters against the accepted manuscript before describing this as a final V1.0 edition. The active V2.0 research is separate and is not included here.
